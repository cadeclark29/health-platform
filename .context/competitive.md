# Competitive Analysis Reference

## CustomDose Positioning
**"The only app that proves whether your supplements actually work -- using your own body's data."**

No single product on the market combines structured supplement tracking with wearable biometric correlation. The market is fragmented: supplement trackers don't connect to wearables, wearable analytics don't understand supplements.

## Key Differentiation Features (What Nobody Else Does)

1. **Closed-Loop Supplement Optimization** - Continuous feedback: log supplements -> measure biometrics -> show what works
2. **N-of-1 Experiment Engine** - Structured self-experiments with baseline/intervention/washout and statistical significance
3. **Ingredient-Level Correlation** - Decompose multi-ingredient products, identify which ingredient drives the effect
4. **Timing Optimization** - Recommend optimal dosing times based on individual sleep/activity patterns
5. **Community-Powered Insights** - Anonymized aggregate: "1,247 similar users saw X effect from Y"
6. **Supplement ROI Calculator** - "You spend $127/mo. These 3 work. These 4 don't. Save $68/mo."

## Monetization Strategy

### Primary: Freemium Subscription
| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Free | $0 | 5 supplements, basic reminders, Oura summary, limited insights |
| Pro | $8-10/mo or $60-80/yr | Unlimited tracking, full correlation analytics, N-of-1 experiments, AI recs, timing optimization, barcode scan |
| Premium | $15-20/mo or $120-150/yr | Everything + community insights, advanced stats, provider export |

### Secondary Revenue
- Affiliate supplement sales (10-15% commission, only recommend what data shows works)
- Anonymized aggregate data licensing to supplement manufacturers/researchers
- B2B practitioner tier ($29-49/mo per practitioner)
- Brand partnerships (featured in protocol library, access to efficacy data)

## Strategic Risks & Timeline

| Risk | Urgency |
|------|---------|
| **Fullscript-Oura Q2 2026** | Must be in market before this launches. Validates the space but captures practitioner mindshare. |
| **Oura expanding native supplement features** | Their Tags system is primitive today, but $1B revenue = resources to build. Establish user base + data moat first. |
| **SuppCo adding wearable integration** | Has best supplement database + quality scoring. If they add Oura/WHOOP, formidable competitor. |
