# Supplement-Wearable Research Reference

Evidence-graded data for supplements trackable via Oura Ring metrics.

## Evidence Strength Key
- **Strong** = Multiple meta-analyses, consistent results
- **Moderate** = Several RCTs, generally positive
- **Weak** = Limited trials, mixed results

---

## Resting Heart Rate

| Supplement | Direction | Dose | Evidence |
|-----------|-----------|------|----------|
| Omega-3 (DHA) | RHR DOWN 2-3bpm | 1-4g/day | Strong (51 RCTs meta-analysis) |
| Magnesium | RHR modestly DOWN (especially during sleep) | 300-400mg/day | Moderate |
| CoQ10 | No significant effect | N/A | Weak/Null |
| Beetroot/Nitrates | BP DOWN (SBP -3.5-5mmHg), RHR unchanged | 250-500mL juice/day | Strong (BP), Null (HR) |
